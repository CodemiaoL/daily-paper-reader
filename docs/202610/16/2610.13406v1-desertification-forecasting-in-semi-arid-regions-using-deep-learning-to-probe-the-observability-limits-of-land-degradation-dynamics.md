## 摘要
对荒漠化的预测受到的关注远少于对其制图。我们使用一个卷积长短期记忆网络（ConvLSTM），在巴西半干旱的塞阿拉州、约2.2 km分辨率的256×206网格上，用2000—2023年的十个年度地球观测（EO）变量进行训练，以衡量源自MapBiomas的退化指数（IDV）的逐年演变有多少可由卫星数据预测。该网络在最后一个观测状态之上预测一个有界变化，分两个阶段训练（单步，然后递归展开），并在一个连续的空间和时间留出集上评估。全州平均IDV从2000年的0.238升至2023年的0.350，约每年0.005，但像元尺度的变化由逐年跳跃主导，而一个使用所有通道的梯度提升模型仅能解释其方差的4.7%。相对于这一上限，ConvLSTM在一个留出的南部条带中，将提前一年的RMSE相对于持续性预测降低1.6%，提前四年降低4.4%（p < 0.001），并优于带恒定漂移的持续性预测，再现了区域漂移的53%—69%。漂移感知基线仅从第三年起才有帮助，在第六年时RMSE最多改善4.4%。一个较早的方案每年重绘整个场，预测的变化与观测变化无关（相关系数-0.06），并在递归运行时产生虚假下降。通道剔除实验表明，高程和EVI会降低预测性能，而坡度和坡向没有可测量的影响。蒙特卡洛dropout和随机情景图传达了期望值预测所隐藏的不确定性。在气候强迫下，平均IDV从2023年的0.350升至2030年的0.370。因此，基于EO的荒漠化预测对区域趋势可靠，但对单个像元并不可靠。

## Abstract
Forecasting desertification has received far less attention than mapping it. We use a convolutional LSTM (ConvLSTM) trained on ten annual Earth Observation (EO) variables (2000-2023) over the semi-arid state of Ceará, Brazil, on a 256x206 grid at roughly 2.2 km resolution, to measure how much of the year-to-year evolution of a MapBiomas-derived degradation index (IDV) can be predicted from satellite data. The network predicts a bounded change on top of the last observed state, is trained in two stages (single-step, then recursive rollout) and is evaluated on a contiguous spatial and temporal holdout. State-wide mean IDV rose from 0.238 to 0.350 between 2000 and 2023, about 0.005 per year, but cell-level change is dominated by year-to-year jumps, and a gradient-boosting model given all channels explains only 4.7% of its variance. Against this ceiling, the ConvLSTM lowers RMSE relative to persistence by 1.6% one year ahead and 4.4% four years ahead in a held-out southern band (p < 0.001), beats persistence with a constant drift and reproduces 53-69% of the regional drift. Drift-aware baselines only help from the third year onward, by up to 4.4% in RMSE at six years. An earlier formulation that redrew the whole field each year predicted changes unrelated to the observed ones (correlation -0.06) and produced a spurious decline when run recursively. Channel knockouts suggest that elevation and EVI degrade the forecast, while slope and aspect have no measurable effect. Monte Carlo dropout and stochastic scenario maps convey the uncertainty that the expected-value forecast hides. Under climatological forcing, mean IDV rises from 0.350 in 2023 to 0.370 in 2030. EO-based desertification forecasting is therefore reliable for the regional trend, not for individual cells.
